App Guide, Safety Rules & Legal
📖 How Foundly Works (User Guide)
  • 1. Submit a Report: Tap "I Lost Something" or "I Found Something", add a clear photo, item title, and last seen area.
  • 2. Smart Matching: Tap "Check Matches" on your report card to view opposite reports in the same category across all locations.
  • 3. Share Missing Poster: For lost items/persons, use the "Download" button to save an official missing poster and share it on WhatsApp or local groups.
  • 4. Close Report: Once your item is recovered, delete your report from the dashboard.
🛡️ Safety & Handover Rules
  • Meet in Public Places: Always exchange items at safe, crowded public spots (police station, mall, metro station, or bank).
  • Verify Ownership First: Before handing over a found item, ask the claimant for proof (purchase bill, serial number, lock-screen wallpaper, or unique marks).
  • No Advance Payments: Never send money online for courier charges or rewards before seeing and verifying your item in person.
  • Missing Persons: Always inform local police authorities immediately alongside posting on Foundly.
🔒 Privacy Policy

Information We Collect: Foundly collects your name, mobile number, uploaded item/person photos, and last seen location solely to match lost and found reports.

How Data is Used: Your contact number is displayed on matched reports so genuine finders or owners can reach you. We never sell or rent your personal data to third-party advertisers.

Data Control: You can delete individual reports anytime from your dashboard or erase all your data from the Profile tab.

📜 Terms & Conditions
  • Foundly acts strictly as a community discovery and matching platform between finders and owners.
  • Users must upload genuine, accurate reports. Fake, spam, or misleading listings will be removed.
  • Foundly is not responsible for item condition, offline disputes, or financial transactions between users.
